The Margin Method

Whether I'm fixing a family calendar or a team project board, I run the same four steps in order.

Identify

I look at what ate Tuesday. Usually it's a recurring meeting with no agenda or a handoff nobody owns.

Eliminate

Cut it before you automate it. Adding an app on top of a broken habit just gives you another inbox to ignore.

Systemize

Write one boring rule you can run without thinking. A 4:50 shutdown and a Sunday Kitchen Sync keep the week from drifting, and parking the phone on the counter protects the weekend.

Reclaim

Take the hour back for dinner or a walk, or finish the real work and close the laptop at 5:00.
