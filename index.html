The Margin Method

I run these four steps on my own week first. Same order every time.

Identify

I look for what ate Tuesday. Usually it's a meeting with no agenda or an endless Slack thread.

Eliminate

I cut it before I automate it. Most “systems” are just another tool on a pile that already doesn't work.

Systemize

Then I write one rule I can run without thinking. Shutting down at 4:50 and a Sunday Kitchen Sync keep the week steady. A phone on the counter and a Digital Sabbath protect the weekend.

Reclaim

The hour goes to dinner or a walk. Or I finish real work and shut the laptop at 5:00.
