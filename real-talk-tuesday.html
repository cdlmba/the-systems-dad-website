[THE REASON THIS EXISTS]

Why it matters right now

My daughter stopped telling me about her day.

I didn't catch the exact Tuesday it happened. I was sitting at the table, but my head was still at my desk. My mind was still grinding through work emails while she was trying to talk to me.

She learned what I was available for. She adjusted accordingly.

I realized then that I wasn't just dropping balls. I was missing the years that matter most.

We don't get these years back. The window goes by much faster than we want to admit.

I write this weekly note as a reminder to look up from the screen and actually be home.

Maybe you need that reminder too.

ISSUE PREVIEW · TUESDAY 5:45 AM

Real talk. I spend all day leading projects and keeping teams organized.

Then I get home—and I am expected to manage the schedule, fix the broken things, and be a great dad all at once.

I thought my system was my memory. Bad idea.

When your plan is "I'll remember to do that tomorrow," you're paying a mental tax every hour. A slice of your brain is permanently occupied—which means you aren't actually there for your kids.

The three things I built to fix it:

  1. The House Board: Stop being the only person who holds the information.
  2. The Sunday Sync: 15 minutes to clear out the noise before the week starts.
  3. The Shutdown Routine: Leave the work brain at your desk.

The result: I'm present for dinner. Mentally. Physically. Fully.

Every issue follows this format. Human first. Routine second. Action always.
