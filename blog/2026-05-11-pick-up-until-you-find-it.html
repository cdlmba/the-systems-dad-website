Pick Up Until You Find It: The 48-Hour Reset

A tool for reclaiming your stewardship when you're stuck in the Executive Residue loop.

I spent years as an academic advisor, hustling my way toward a career I thought I wanted. I was the guy working 80 hours a week, eating Hardee’s for lunch in my car because I "didn't have time" for a break, pouring everything into an employer who didn't even know my daughters' names.

I thought loyalty was a two-way street. I found out quickly it only goes one way.

I was on a runaway train, never going back.

I was headbutting a curb, thinking if I just hit it harder, I'd eventually break through.

Instead, I was just breaking myself. My career was being laid out for me by people who didn't share my vision, and my identity was being stolen by a job that would replace me in a week if I dropped dead.

This spilled into my family. I was frazzled, confused, and suffering from a constant hum of anxiety that I couldn't switch off. I needed a reset. I needed to stop digging.

You know the feeling. You pull into the driveway at 5:01 PM on a Friday. You’re exhausted, but you don't go inside. You sit there for five minutes, ten minutes, staring at the steering wheel.

You’re trying to find the "Dad" gear, but all you can feel is the Executive Residue. You’re tired of being tired. This time, it has to be different.

Here's a pivot you can do in a weekend to get back on track.

Friday Night: Clear the Clutter

Goal: Stop the loop. - The Scene: You’re frazzled. You can’t remember a single win from this week. - The Move: Close the laptop. Write down the 3 things lingering in your head. Externalize the anxiety so you can walk through the door with Zero Residue.

Saturday: Calibrate the System

Goal: Ownership. - 8:00 AM - Identity Check: You’ve governed portfolios at work; now it's time to govern your own life. They are integrated. - 8:15 AM - Systems Audit: What is actually lingering? What is broken? Be honest. - 10:15 AM - Systems Research: Don't get fancy. Find the simplest tool for the specific friction point. - 1:15 PM - Systems Design: The new setup. Capture -> Work -> Reference -> Visualize. AI-first, capture-driven. - 3:00 PM - Implementation: Start now. Your success can’t wait until Monday. - 5:00 PM - Commitment: Sign a note to yourself. Define the new identity.

Sunday: Find Your Next Move

Goal: Clarity. - 8:00 AM - The Sabbath Gear: Counter-intuitive rest. Log off. A system of rest is still a system. - 12:00 PM - Identity Manifestation: Write out your "New Normal." Who are you becoming? - 4:00 PM - The Escape Room: Look for the patterns. Look for the clues. You’ve found the way out of the maze. - 5:00 PM - SC-15: The 15-minute Sunday Calibration. Win the week before it starts.

There is power in setting up a structure and defining a new identity. You aren't "fixing your schedule." You are reclaiming your stewardship.

Are you ready for a reset to be the man and father you know you want to be?
