Why most AI setups get abandoned by Wednesday

"Agents? I have no patience to set up or maintain them."

A blank chat window doesn't remember how you work unless you write your rules down in one file first. Keeping a workflow running is where people quit anyway, because one person still has to own checking the brief before it goes out (as these tools are right now).
