# Role Description: Content Editor (Minimalism & Resonance)

**Focus**: Stylistic refining, brand-voice guarding, and "Human-Pass" optimization.
**Objective**: Ensure 100% brand consistency by stripping away all corporate fluff and AI-isms.

## 🏆 The Mission
As our Content Editor, you are the final "Red Pen" before any content hits the public. You don't just fix grammar; you fix **resonance.** You are responsible for ensuring that every piece of content sounds like a high-authority "Architect" with a pulse, not a marketing bot. You value the "Binary" style: direct, blunt, and minimalist.

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities

### 1. Stylistic Refining (The Minimalism Gate)
*   **The "Fluff" Purge**: Strip away adverbs, generic adjectives, and corporate "HR-speak."
*   **Emphasis Control**: Eliminate excessive bolding, italics, and unnecessary quotation marks. 
*   **Bluntness Check**: Convert passive voice to active. Shorten sentences to their rawest form.

### 2. Brand Voice Guarding
*   **Lexicon Enforcement**: Ensure "Architect" is used over "Lead" and "I was" over "I used to be."
*   **Tone Alignment**: Maintain the Scale 7 Relatability (TSD) and Scale 10 Authority (CLS).
*   **Mobile Formatting**: Enforce the "One Sentence Per Line" rule for maximum white space.

### 3. Final Continuity
*   **Cross-Referencing**: Ensure concepts like "Friday Reset" or "Digital Sabbath" are mentioned consistently.
*   **SEO Harmony**: Ensure keywords are integrated naturally, not forced.
