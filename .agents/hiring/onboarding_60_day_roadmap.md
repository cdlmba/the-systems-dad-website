# Onboarding Roadmap: 60 Days to Content Velocity

**Goal**: Seamless transition of the "Content Desk" from Christopher to the new hire, reaching a cadence of 1+ Guild applications per week.

## 🏗 Phase 1: Brand & System Immersion (Days 1–15)
*Focus: Understanding the "Operator-Human" frequency.*

*   **Audit**: Review the last 6 months of "Tactical Tuesday" and LinkedIn posts.
*   **Skill Mastery**: Learn the [Systems Dad Brand Knowledge Base](file:///c:/Users/Test/OneDrive/Documents/1%20Projects/104%20Christopher%20Lynn%20Systems%20Website/Skills/Knowledge/systems-dad-brand-knowledge.md) and [CMO Marketing Governance](file:///c:/Users/Test/OneDrive/Documents/1%20Projects/104%20Christopher%20Lynn%20Systems%20Website/.agents/skills/cmo-marketing-governance/SKILL.md).
*   **Technical Setup**: Connect to the Discord System Hub and Obsidian Workspace.
*   **Deliverable**: A "Brand Voice Audit" exercise where the hire rewrites 3 generic AI posts into the "Systems Dad" voice.

## 🚀 Phase 2: The Repurposing Sprint (Days 16–45)
*Focus: Executing the internal multiplication engine.*

*   **Drafting**: Take the live "Tactical Tuesday" and draft the social distribution loop (3x LinkedIn, 2x X) in Obsidian.
*   **Manual Review Loop**: Christopher reviews every post before scheduling. Feedback is recorded to refine the "Brand Linter."
*   **Blog Engine**: Own the `build_blog.py` process. Ensure every newsletter becomes a permanent on-domain blog post.
*   **Deliverable**: 4 consecutive weeks of 100% content distribution adherence.

## 🎯 Phase 3: The Growth Pivot (Days 46–60)
*Focus: External reach and optimization.*

*   **Outreach Layer**: Identify 10 high-authority podcasts and draft the initial "Pitch Kit" for Christopher.
*   **SEO Audit**: Optimize the top 5 performing blog posts for higher search intent.
*   **The 1+ Goal**: Actively track the source of every new Guild application.
*   **Deliverable**: The first qualified Guild application attributed to organic distribution.
