# Onboarding Roadmap: 60 Days to Stylistic Mastery

**Goal**: Establish a "Zero-Friction" content flow where the Editor perfectly captures the CEO's minimalist brand voice.

## 🏗 Phase 1: Immersion & Red-Penning (Days 1–15)
*Focus: Internalizing the "Binary" Style.*

*   **Audit**: Review the last 10 blog posts and compare the "AI Drafts" to the "CEO Final Edits."
*   **Skill Mastery**: Internalize the new minimalist standards (No caps/bolding bloat, "Architect" vs "Lead").
*   **The "Shadow" Edit**: Take 3 new drafts and perform a "Silent Edit" for review by the CMO.
*   **Deliverable**: A "Style Guide Appendix" that documents the CEO's specific word-choice preferences.

## 🚀 Phase 2: The Editing Sprint (Days 16–45)
*Focus: Owning the "Final Pass."*

*   **Workflow Integration**: Take raw drafts from the **Content Multiplier** and perform the "Red-Pen" pass.
*   **Automation**: Set up a "Brand Linter" checklist in Obsidian for every piece of content.
*   **Manual Feedback Loop**: Review every manual change made by the CEO to refine the "Minimalism Gate."
*   **Deliverable**: 4 consecutive weeks of 100% adherence to mobile-first formatting with zero brand drift.

## 🎯 Phase 3: Brand Authority (Days 46–60)
*Focus: High-Impact Polish.*

*   **External Harmony**: Ensure LinkedIn posts and Blog posts share the same "Blunt" resonance.
*   **SEO Optimization**: Fine-tune meta descriptions and hooks for maximum click-through rate without sacrificing brand tone.
*   **The 5% Goal**: Reach the milestone where manual CEO edits are no longer required for distribution.
*   **Deliverable**: A "Published" portfolio of 10+ posts with 0% corporate fluff.

---

> [!TIP]
> **The Tony Fadell Rule**: The CEO will "Zoom In" for these 60 days to give context on the "Subtle" part of the voice. Success is defined by the Editor's ability to anticipate the "Cut."
