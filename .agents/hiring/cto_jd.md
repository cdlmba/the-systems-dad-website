# Role Description: Fractional CTO (Architecture & Quality)

**Focus**: Technical debt reduction, modular architecture, and code standards for CLS/TSD.
**Objective**: Transition the monolithic codebase into a scalable "Cloudflare Worker Core" architecture.

## 🏆 The Mission
As our Fractional CTO, you are the guardian of technical integrity. You aren't just here to write code; you are here to architect the systems that allow us to scale without collapsing under technical debt. You will transform our current experimental scripts and monolithic Worker into a professional-grade edge platform.

## 🎯 Key Responsibilities

### 1. Architectural Evolution (Cloudflare Worker Core)
*   **Monolith Decomposition**: Lead the refactoring of `src/index.js` into modular, testable components.
*   **Library Advisory**: Evaluate and advise on the adoption of routing frameworks (e.g., Hono) vs. custom logic.
*   **API Design**: Standardize the communication between our frontend assets and the back-end logic.

### 2. Code Quality & Governance
*   **The Technical Debt Backlog**: Identify, track, and prioritize the remediation of "legacy" hacks in the build system and Worker.
*   **Standardization**: Enforce ES6+ standards, consistent naming conventions, and JSDoc documentation.
*   **Automation**: Implement automated linting and pre-commit checks as quality gates.

### 3. Performance & Security
*   **Latency Monitoring**: Ensure Worker execution stays within optimal CPU/Memory limits.
*   **Edge Optimization**: Optimize asset delivery and caching logic to maintain "Scale 10 Authority" performance.

## 📊 Success Metrics (First 90 Days)
*   **Worker Modularity**: 100% of business logic extracted from the main `fetch` handler into dedicated modules.
*   **Tech Debt Velocity**: Resolution of the "Top 3" high-friction areas identified in the initial audit.
*   **System Stability**: 0 regressions during the transition to the new Worker core.

## 🛠 Required Stack
*   **Cloudflare Workers/Wrangler**: Expert-level edge computing.
*   **JavaScript (ESNext)**: Deep knowledge of asynchronous patterns and functional programming.
*   **Python**: For legacy build script oversight.
*   **GitHub/CI**: For managing quality gates.
