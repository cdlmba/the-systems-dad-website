# Markdown Allowance Engine

Use this template to architect the "work-reward" cycle in your home. 

A Strategic Lead doesn't just "give" money to kids. They build a system that teaches them how to manage capacity, handle responsibility, and understand leverage (the "Kid OS").

### **The "Work-Reward" Architecture**

Break down the household contribution into three tiers:

| Tier | Definition | Examples | Reward |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Citizenship** | Non-negotiable baseline for living in the house. | Making bed, clearing plate. | $0 (Participation) |
| **Operational** | Recurring tasks that keep the gears turning. | Trash, dishes, pet care. | Base Allowance |
| **Leverage Tasks**| One-off "Architecture" projects or heavy lifting. | Clearing the garage, sorting files. | One-Off Bonus |

### **The "Kid OS" Sync (Weekly)**

During your **Weekly Sync**, have a 5-minute "Finance Check-In" with the kids.

**The "Bottom Line" (Financial Split)**:
*   *Spend*: (e.g., Immediate small wins).
*   *Save*: (e.g., Toward a medium-term goal).
*   *Give*: (e.g., Intentional generosity).

**The "Maintenance" Scan**:
*   *Checklist Audit*: Were all the citizenship/operational tasks done?
*   *Logic Gaps*: Where did the "Work" fail this week?
*   *Future Prep*: What are the big events (e.g., Birthday party) next week?

> [!TIP]
> **Systems Dad Pro-Tip**: If you find yourself nagging your kids to do their chores, your system is broken. Change the architecture so the system does the nagging for you.
