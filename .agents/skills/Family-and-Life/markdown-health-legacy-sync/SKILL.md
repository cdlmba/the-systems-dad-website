# Markdown Health Legacy Sync

Use this template to architect your own health and longevity. 

A Strategic Lead knows they can't protect the "heart" of the house if the "engine" is broken. Your physical and mental capacity is the most important lever for your family's future. 

### **The "Engine" Scan (The Basics)**

Track the non-negotiables for high-capacity leadership:

| Gear | Audit Metric | Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sleep** | Duration + Quality | 7+ Hours |
| **Movement** | Physical activity | 30+ Minutes |
| **Nutrition** | Clean fuel % | 80% Rule |
| **Hydration** | Water intake | 2L+ |

### **The "State check" (Mental Capacity)**

*   **Stress Load**: (1-10) *How reactive was I today?*
*   **Presence Level**: (1-10) *Was I fully at the baseball game?*
*   **Logic Gap Fix**: *What stole my capacity today? (e.g., A 9 PM email).*

### **The "Legacy" Outcome**

**The "Bottom Line" (Personal Health Goal)**:
*I am protecting my health so I can... (e.g., Be active for my grandkids, lead my team with energy).*

**The "Maintenance" Ritual**:
*   *Strategic Shutdown*: ALARM at (e.g., 10 PM) to ensure sleep.
*   *Morning Win*: One physical win before the house wakes up.
