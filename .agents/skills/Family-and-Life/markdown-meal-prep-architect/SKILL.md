# Markdown Meal Prep Architect

Use this template to architect the "fuel" of your home. 

A Strategic Lead doesn't just "ask what's for dinner." They build a system of **Anchor Meals** and **Grocery Syncs** to eliminate the daily 5 PM "firefighting" during the witching hour.

*   **The "Prep Logic"**: What can be done on Sunday to buy back 30 minutes on Wednesday?
*   **The "Hub Link"**: Is the grocery list in the **Information Hub**?

*   Phones are in the "Sabbath State" (Off the table).
