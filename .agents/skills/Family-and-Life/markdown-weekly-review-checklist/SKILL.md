# Weekly Review Checklist (Systems Edition)

## Use this skill when
- It's the end of the week (Friday afternoon or Sunday evening) and you need to reset
- You feel overwhelmed by open loops or "mental clutter"
- You want to align your next week with your long-term systems and family goals

## Step-by-step instructions
1. **Clear the Decks**:
   - Empty Physical Inboxes (mail, receipts, business cards)
   - Empty Digital Inboxes (Email, Slack, Downloads folder)
   - "Brain Dump": Write down every open loop in your mind
2. **Get Current**:
   - Review Calendar (last 2 weeks and next 4 weeks)
   - Review Waiting For list (follow up as needed)
   - Review Projects list (ensure every project has a "Next Action")
3. **Get Creative**:
   - Review "Someday/Maybe" list
   - Review "Dad-Life" goals (adventures, family systems, legacy)
   - Identify the "One Big Thing" for next week
4. **Format output**:
   - Provide the review in a clean, checkable Markdown format
   - Include a "Systems Audit" section for recurring automations

## Best practices
- Keep it practical: Focus on what *actually* moves the needle
- Incorporate "The Systems Dad" tone: Practical, humble, and value-driven
- Suggest 15-30 minutes for the full process
