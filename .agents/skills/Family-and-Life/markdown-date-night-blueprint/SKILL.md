# Markdown Date Night Blueprint

Use this blueprint to architect an intentional connection with your spouse.

A Strategic Lead doesn't just "show up." They prepare for the session to ensure they move from "Administrative Logistics" to "Deep Connection."

### **The "Pre-Flight" Prep (30 Seconds)**

Before the date begins, clear the runway:

*   **Confirm the Logistics**: Sitter, reservations, and transport are 100% handled.
*   **The "Digital Hand-Off"**: Turn off work notifications. Put the phone in the "Glove Box" state (emergencies only).
*   **The "State Check"**: Shift from "Manager" to "Partner."

### **The Connection Architecture (The Conversation)**

Move beyond the "Gears" (the calendar/chores) and focus on the "Heart."

| Stage | Intentional Topic |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wins of the Week** | What was the highlight of your week? Where did you feel most supported? |
| **The "North Star" Sync**| How are we doing on our **Family Mission**? What feels out of sync? |
| **Future Vision** | What are you most excited about for next month/year? |
| **The "Leverage" Question**| What is the one thing I can do to buy you more margin this week? |

### **The Outcome: Reclaimed Intimacy**

**The "High-Leverage" Takeaway**:
*One thing we decided or realized tonight:*

**The "Future Sync"**:
*Date Night Scheduled For*: (Next Week)
