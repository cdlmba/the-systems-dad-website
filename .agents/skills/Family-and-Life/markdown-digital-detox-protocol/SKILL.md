# Markdown Digital Detox Protocol

Use this protocol to transition from "Work Brain" to "Home Brain." 

A Strategic Lead knows that high-leverage work requires high-leverage rest. If your brain is constantly "syncing" with the office, you aren't winning at home.

### **The "Sabbath" Countdown (Friday Evening)**

Clear the gears so the heart can flourish:

*   **The "Retro" Sync**: Have you completed your **Weekly Retro**? If no, do it now.
*   **The "Loose Ends" Dump**: Move any 4 PM Friday ideas into your **Information Hub** for Monday. 
*   **The Notifications Kill-Switch**: Turn off work emails, Slack, and LinkedIn notifications.

### **The "Digital Sabbath" Logic**

Choose your level of **Systemic Rest**:

| Mode | Action | Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Grounded** | Phone in a designated drawer for 24 hours. | Maximum presence. |
| **Glove-Box State**| Phone available only for calls/texts from "Family Favorites." | Peace of mind with safety. |
| **Offline Sync** | No social media or business research for 24 hours. | Reclaims mental cycles. |

### **The "Presence" Blueprint (The Sabbath Activities)**

What will you do with the 24 hours of reclaimed margin?

*   *Team Play*: (e.g., Baseball with the kids, park trip).
*   *Analog Mastery*: (e.g., Reading a physical book, cooking a meal).
*   *Deep Rest*: (e.g., A nap that doesn't feel like a "failure").
