# Systems Dad Article Image Generator

## Use this skill when
- You’ve finished an article and need a "stop-the-scroll" cover image
- You need a product thumbnail for Whop that feels premium and trustworthy
- You want a consistent, high-end visual brand across all your content

## Step-by-step instructions

1. **Identify the Core Visual Metaphor**: 
   - Is it about *Friction*? (e.g., tangled wires, a cluttered desk)
   - *Leverage*? (e.g., a simple focal point, a single tool, a high-end watch)
   - *Connection*? (e.g., a family focus, warm light, a shared meal)
   - *System Builders*? (e.g., a sleek laptop, glassmorphic UI, a blueprint)

2. **Select Your Aesthetic Bucket**:
   - **Modern Technical**: Glassmorphism, deep slates, minimalist electronics.
   - **Tactile Leadership**: Leather-bound journals, oak desks, high-end pens.
   - **Home-Front Winning**: Warm sunset light, subtle family elements, soft focus.

3. **Populate the Prompt Recipe**:
   - **Subject**: [Main object or scene]
   - **Setting**: [The environment, e.g., a minimalist's home office at sunset]
   - **Vibe**: [Cinematic, calm, professional, high-leverage]
   - **Color Palette**: [Deep slate, gold, warm oak, minimalist white]
   - **Brand Constraint**: **NO TEXT.**

## The "Standard" Systems Dad Prompt Template

Use the following as a base for AI image generation:

> "A premium, high-fidelity cover image for an article titled [TITLE]. The scene is a [SETTING]. A [SUBJECT] is the focal point, designed with a [AESTHETIC BUCKET] feel. The lighting is [cinematic/warm/natural]. High-contrast textures (leather, metal, wood). Curated color palette: [COLORS]. Professional, sophisticated, and high-leverage. NO TEXT."

## Best practices
- **Avoid Stock Clichés**: No generic "office" or "handshake" photos.
- **Lighting is King**: Use "warm morning light" or "cinematic sunset" to avoid flat images.
- **Texture Matters**: Detail the leather of a journal or the glass of a screen.
- **Brand Consistency**: Always mention "Deep Slate" and "Warm Wood" for that signature Systems Dad look.
- **Always specify 'No Text'**: Most AI generators struggle with text; keep it clean and professional.
