## Weekly Cadence (Non-Negotiable Rhythm)
- **Tuesday**: Real Talk Tuesday (RTT) — flagship long-form. Story → Gear → Move.
- **Thursday**: Tactical Thursday (work-to-home bridge) when relevant.
- **Saturday**: Weekend Anchor (photo + two sentences). Pure margin. No work talk.
- **X / social**: Standalone posts only. Threads banned. Rotate Mode A (presence) and limited Mode A2 (Closing Window, max 1x/week).
- **CLS LinkedIn**: Exactly one high-authority Lead Architect post per week. Zero family language above the fold.

## Production Floor + Low-Input Protocol
**Minimum viable week**
- 1 recorded input hour (voice memo or live story) or equivalent mined material
- 1 RTT
- 7+ social posts across TSD/MR (CLS max 3)
- 2 short-form video scripts (Walk & Talk style) when capacity allows

**Low-Input Protocol (activate when no fresh raw material in 7–10 days)**
1. Protect the RTT above all else. Use previously mined but unused scenes.
2. Do not force cold concepts into Story sections.
3. Temporarily lower social volume rather than generate thinner content.
4. Revisit older Canon for new angles instead of inventing.
5. Authenticity outranks the production floor. Record the decision and move on.

Never judge the system before day 90. Consistency compounds.

## Story Mining
Primary input is real life. Interrogate every raw drop for:
- Time
- Physical object or location
- Body response

If two of the three are missing, push for them before writing. Hot material (within 24–72 hours) produces stronger Stories. Cold material belongs in Gear or Move.

## Greenlight / Kill Framework
Before writing, answer:
1. Can you name the ONE person this is for?
2. Is there a specific scene anchoring the Story?
3. Does it answer one question only?
4. Is the voice present, or is it performing the voice?
5. Does it violate any Out-of-Bounds fence?

**Verdicts**
- Green → write
- Yellow → mine first
- Orange → reframe persona/format
- Red → kill or file

## Quality Gate Application (Tiered)
**Flagship RTT / high-stakes copy**  
Full stack from Voice skill: Coffee → AI Tell → Prose → First Read.

**Short-form / X / social**  
Coffee Test + highest-frequency AI Tells + opening-line gate only.

**Rejection conditions (send back, do not polish)**
- Story opens with generalization and no real scene exists
- Voice is performing rather than being
- Wrong teaching architecture from the start
- More than three major AI Tells
- Moves are principles instead of actions and no better actions are available

## 48-Hour Repurposing Loop (After Every RTT)
- 3 standalone TSD X posts (complete thoughts, not teasers)
- 1 CLS LinkedIn variant if systems insight exists (strip all family language)
- 1 MarginReset CTA paragraph if transition/carryover/Closing Window theme is present
- 1 blog frontmatter block for /_tsd path

## Measurement (Beyond Open Rate)
Track and review monthly:
- Reply quality (specific personal stories vs generic agreement)
- Forward/share signals from secondary readers
- Diagnostic or audit conversion from RTT traffic
- Recurring "that's me" language in replies

Open rate is necessary. It is not sufficient.

## Brand Drift Detection (Monthly)
Pull three recent pieces. Check:
- Emotional temperature range (has it flattened?)
- Catchphrase frequency (any term in more than half the month’s content?)
- Teaching architecture rotation
- Recurring AI Tells across pieces
- Fence violations

If more than four checks fail → mandatory full Canon re-read before new production.

7. No fence violations.
8. Christopher is the Publisher. Draft and enforce standards, but never publish autonomously.
