## Content Standards (The X Punch)

## 🧠 The Mandatory Three-Beat Framework (COH)

Every post or thread must run through these three beats in order to maintain the brand's unique mix of vulnerability, authority, and relatability.

1. **Chaos (Vulnerability – 40-50%)**: Start with the raw, specific mess you created or lived. Name the feeling of being physically present but mentally a ghost.
   - *Example*: “I was the dad walking through my own house like a ghost. Physically there, but replaying Slack threads while my kids tried to show me their drawings…” 
   - No vague “life is busy.” Be specific and mention the burnout cost.

2. **Operator (Skill Transfer – 30%)**: Show how you brought your program manager / systems brain home. Bridge it clearly: “So I did what I do at the office…” 
   - Bridge it clearly: “As a PM who organizes chaos at work, I used system design to build margin I didn't think existed…” 
   - Explain the principle or system applied without being salesy.

3. **Human (Real-Dad Humanity – 20-30%)**: End with imperfection. No "guru polish."
   - *Example*: “It helps most days. I still stumble. But the margin is there, and I’m showing up again.”
   - Avoid the “built different” vibe. Stay relatable.

### 2. The Soulful Voice (No AI Slop)
- **Avoid**: "Harnessing," "Pivotal," "Landscape," "Tapestry," "Delve."
- **Inject**: Specific failures and vulnerability. "I've been there, here's the system I built."
- **Vibe**: Helpful big brother. Authoritative but human.

### 3. The Vertical Scroll (One Thought Per Line)
- **Formatting**: Double line breaks after every punchline. 
- **Mobile-First**: Optimized for a 3-second scroll stop.

## 📈 Refined 2026 Action Plan (@TheSystemsDad)

Stick to your superpower: **Systems thinking for ambitious dads/tech PMs.** Blend in timely hooks when natural, but anchor everything in value + systems.

### 1. Content Formula That Wins
- **Hook**: Start with a pain point or hot take.
- **Insight**: Provide a systems insight or quick win.
- **Visual**: Use a screenshot, simple chart, or before/after comparison.
- **Engagement**: Ask a question or use a poll for replies.

### 2. Strategic Optimization Checklist
- **Always Add Media**: Images boost impressions 2-5x. Use screenshots of Notion templates, simple Canva graphics, or workflow diagrams.
- **Posting Cadence**: Max 3x/day. At least 1 strong original post. Time for US mornings/evenings (high-intent windows).
- **Hard Engagement**: Reply to every comment + 10 relevant replies to larger accounts in #Productivity, #ProductManagement, and #SystemsThinking.
- **Threads > Singles**: Threads multiply impressions across each tweet.
- **Pin a Winner**: Regularly update your pinned post to your best-performing systems thread.

### 3. High-Leverage Content Ideas

## 🧭 The Niche Coherence Protocol (Algorithm Alignment)

Maintain a clean semantic profile for Grok and your audience. Every post should reinforce your authority as **The Systems Dad.**

1. **The 30-Tweet Audit**: Regularly check your niche coherence. Go through your last 30 tweets. Do they all clearly point to the same thing? Or are you all over the place? Make sure your semantic profile is clean and obvious.
2. **Selective Engagement**: Cut your comment volume. Stop dropping generic comments on 50 posts a day thinking it builds reach. It's doing the opposite now. Be selective. Be useful. Or stay quiet.
3. **Quality > Quantity**: Post less, write better. The quantity game is dead for now. One well-constructed, niche-coherent post is worth more than five mediocre ones. Treat each post like it costs you something.
4. **Niche Reframing**: Don't fight the profile. If you want to post about something off-niche, either reframe it through the lens of your niche (Systems/Fatherhood/TPM) or don't post it on your main feed. Simple.
