# Case Study Blueprinter: Systems Dad Version

Use this skill to convert a technical win (AI adoption, program overhaul, or automation build) into a high-leverage case study. These assets build authority with professional peers while showing the "Systems Dad" ethos in action.

## The Case Study Blueprint

| Section | Focus | The "Systems Dad" Narrative |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The Result Hook** | The big outcome. | Start with the metrics. e.g., "Reclaiming 15 hours for a Tech PM." |
| **2. The Logic Gap** | The messy status quo. | Describe the "Manual Sprint" or the "Operational Drag" before the fix. |
| **3. The System Audit** | Why the current way failed. | Apply the **60% Rule**. Show how even high-achievers were capped by a bad system. |
| **4. The Systems Dad Fix** | The technical implementation. | Document the "Lever." What was automated? What was standardized? |
| **5. The Transformation** | Life after the system. | The combination of professional ROI and the "Win at Home" (Presence). |

## Writing Standards (The "Authority" Pass)

### 1. High-Stakes Tone (No AI Slop)
- **Forbidden**: "Harnessing," "Pivotal," "Transformative," "Tapestry," "Journey."
- **Inject**: Specific tools (Zapier, Copilot, Notion), clear metrics, and project management jargon (backlog, velocity, bottlenecks).
- **Vibe**: Direct, analytical, and results-oriented.

### 2. The "Before vs. After" Contrast
- Use side-by-side lists or clear H2 contrasts to show the difference between "Chaos" and "Order."
- Ensure the "After" state explicitly mentions reclaimed time for family/rest.

### 3. Proof Points
- **Metrics**: Time saved, revenue increased, or error rates dropped.
- **Testimonial**: If available, use the "Voice of the Stakeholder" (even if that stakeholder is yourself).

## Creative Process
1. **Define the Victory**: What was the specific technical or operational win?
2. **Audit the Failure**: Why was the old way unsustainable? (The 60% Rule).
3. **Draft the Implementation**: Map out the specific automations or protocols used.
4. **Link to Presence**: How did this win specifically help the "Home Side"?

## Output Requirement
1. **The Full Case Study**: Formatted in Markdown (ready for Blog or Portfolio).
2. **The "Results Summary" Card**: A punchy 3-bullet summary for LinkedIn headers.
3. **The Call-to-Action**: A natural lead-in to a Systems Audit or the 5-Day Reset.

Now, blueprint the case study based on the project details provided.
