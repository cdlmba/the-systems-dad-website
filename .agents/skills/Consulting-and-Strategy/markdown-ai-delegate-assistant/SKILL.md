# Markdown AI Delegate Assistant

Use these prompts to turn your rough thoughts into clear delegation scripts.

A Strategic Lead doesn't just "pass off" work. They architect a hand-off that ensures success and buys back capacity.

### **The "Director-Level" Polisher**

Use this when you need to send a high-stakes status update to a Director or VP.

> I have some rough notes for a status update. 
> 
> My notes: [PASTE NOTES]
> 
> Help me polish this into a clear, strategic update for a Director. 
> 
> Follow the **Systems Dad** brand voice:
> 1. **Lead with the Outcome**: What is the "bottom line" result?
> 2. **Highlight the Systems Gain**: How did we reduce friction or build capacity?
> 3. **Be Direct**: Active voice. Short sentences. No jargon. No em-dashes.
> 4. **Tone**: Upbeat, strategic, and encouraging. Use plain language.

### **The "Clear Delegation" Script**

Use this when you are assigning a task to a team member or a virtual assistant.

> I need to delegate a task. 
> 
> The task: [DESCRIBE TASK]
> 
> Draft a delegation message that follows these principles:
> 1. **Explain the "Why"**: How does this task help us lead at work and win at home?
> 2. **Define the Definition of Done**: What does a perfect "Blueprint" look like for this?
> 3. **Identify the Logic Gaps**: What are the most likely "turbulence" points the person might hit?
> 4. **Tone**: Helpful big-brother. Practical and humble. Use contractions. Support the team.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Systems Dad Pro-Tip**: Delegation isn't "abdication." It's operational architecture.
