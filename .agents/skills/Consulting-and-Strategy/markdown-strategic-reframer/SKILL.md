# Markdown Strategic Reframer

Use this prompt when you hit a breakdown in your day or week. 

It helps you move from being a "firefighter" to a "strategic lead."

Instead of just fixing the current issue, you'll architect a system that prevents it from ever happening again.

### **The Prompt**

Copy the text below into Microsoft Copilot or Google Gemini.

---

> I have just encountered a "reactive fire" in my day. 
> 
> Here is the situation: [DESCRIBE THE ISSUE BRIEFLY]
> 
> Help me use the **Strategic Reframer** protocol to buy back my capacity. 
> 
> Based on the **Systems Dad** brand philosophy, please:
> 
> 1. **Analyze the Friction**: What was the specific logic gap or system failure that allowed this fire to start? 
> 2. **Reframe the Problem**: Instead of a "one-off mistake," treat this as a signal of a broken system.
> 3. **Architect the Solution**: Draft a simple, high-leverage system (e.g., a checklist, an automation, or a recurring sync) to solve this for good.
> 4. **Identify the Leverage**: How much margin will this build into my week if I never have to deal with it again?
> 
> Speak like a helpful big-brother. Use plain language. Be upbeat and encouraging. Avoid jargon.
