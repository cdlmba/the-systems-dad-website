# Decision Journal (Systems Thinking Edition)

## Use this skill when
- Making a significant decision (technical, financial, or family-related)
- You want to reflect on your thinking process later
- You're applying "Systems Thinking" to a complex problem

## Step-by-step instructions
1. **The Decision**: What are you deciding? (Clearly state it)
2. **The Context**: Why are you deciding this now?
3. **Alternatives Considered**: What else did you think about? Why were they rejected?
4. **Predictions**: What do you expect to happen? (Make specific, falsifiable claims)
5. **Timeline**: When will you review this decision? (6 months? 1 year?)
6. **Emotions**: How are you feeling right now? (Combatting bias)

## Best practices
- Use H2 for the main sections
- Keep it objective: Stick to facts and documented thoughts
- Use the "Systems Dad" voice (humility + strategic thinking)
- Encourage a "Review" section for 6 months later
