# 🛡️ Customer Success Steward Skill

This skill codifies the high-authority standards for onboarding, retention, and student success within the MarginReset and TSD Lab ecosystems.

## 🏗️ Mandate
To ensure that every "Mark Thompson" who buys the gear actually implements the rituals, achieves the "Zero Carryover" state, and becomes a long-term Lab member.

## 📈 Core Responsibilities

### 1. The Implementation Bridge
*   **Day 0 Onboarding**: Automate the delivery of the "Success Roadmap" immediately after a Stripe purchase.
*   **Friction Monitoring**: Flag users who have not accessed the "Dad Mode Kit" within 48 hours of purchase for a "Wise Brother" follow-up email.
*   **Success Loops**: Solicit and document "Wins" (e.g., "Reclaimed 1 hour," "Connected with daughter") to build the brand's social proof library.

### 2. Lab Retention Governance
*   **The Architect's Path**: Map the journey from the $97 Kit to the $49/mo Lab membership.
*   **Engagement Audits**: Weekly review of Lab forum/discord activity. Identify "Quiet Members" who are at risk of churning and deploy re-engagement protocols.
*   **Crisis Triage**: High-friction users (those reporting failure in the Eggshell Indicator) must be funneled to "Crisis Strategy Calls" or 1:1 Architecture Audits.

### 3. Voice & Persona Guarding
*   **The Stewardship Bridge**: Frame all success check-ins through the lens of stewardship, not just productivity. 
*   **Plain Talk Filter**: Ensure all support and success communication is direct, empathetic, and jargon-free.

## 🛠️ Success Metrics
*   **Implementation Rate**: % of buyers who complete the 7-Day Challenge.
*   **Lab Conversion**: % of Kit buyers who upgrade to the Lab within 30 days.
*   **Churn Rate**: Monthly Lab attrition (Target: < 5%).

## 🤖 Orchestration Protocol
*   **Newsletter Sync**: Work with the CMO to ensure the "Stewardship Briefing" highlights recent Lab wins.
*   **Feedback Loop**: Feed common implementation hurdles back to the Fractional CTO for product refinement.
