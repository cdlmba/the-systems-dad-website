# High-Impact Guide Builder: Systems Dad Version

Use this skill to architect a definitive guide on a complex topic (e.g., "The Complete Guide to Family Logistics" or "AI Agent Governance for Program Managers"). Your goal is to establish "Unicorn-level" authority by being the most comprehensive resource on the web.

## The Guide Blueprint

| Section | Focus | The "Systems Dad" Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. The Introduction** | The "Logic Gap" context. | Why this guide exists and what failure it solves. |
| **2. Core Principles** | The foundational logic. | State the **Founding Principles** (e.g., Clarity-First, Attention as Infrastructure). |
| **3. The Tech Stack** | Tools & Automations. | A breakdown of the specific software and "Levers" required. |
| **4. The Playbook** | Step-by-Step Implementation. | A detailed "How-To" that moves from manual to automated. |
| **5. Friction Points** | Troubleshooting. | Address the common ways people fail or hit the **60% Rule** cap. |
| **6. The Transformation** | The "Presence" outcome. | Reclaim the ROI: Why this lead to a **Win at Home.** |

## Writing Standards (The "Definitive" Pass)

### 1. The Expert Voice (No AI Slop)
- **Forbidden**: "Harnessing," "Pivotal," "Transformative," "Tapestry," "Landscape."
- **Inject**: Deep technical details, specific failure modes, and "Systems Dad" terminology.
- **Vibe**: The ultimate manual. High information density with zero fluff.

### 2. Structured Authority
- Use H1, H2, and H3 headers to create a clear hierarchy.
- Use callout blocks (Quotes or Alerts) for "Systems Moves" and "Dad Wisdom."
- Include checklists or "Resource Hub" sections for value-adds.

### 3. SEO Optimization
- Ensure natural keywords are used in headers.
- Focus on "Problem/Solution" phrasing that users actually search for.

## Creative Process
1. **Define the Scope**: What is the single most important problem this guide solves?
2. **Audit the Principles**: Which "Founding Principles" apply here?
3. **Map the Playbook**: Break the transformation into 5-7 logical steps.
4. **Final Polishing**: Ensure the guide feels like a "premium asset" even in Markdown format.

## Output Requirement
1. **The Full Guide**: Formatted in Markdown (ready for Site or PDF).
2. **The "Wait-What" Summary**: A 5-bullet summary of the core findings.
3. **Downloadable Lead Magnet suggestion**: A concept for a worksheet or checklist.

Now, architect the High-Impact Guide based on the topic provided.
