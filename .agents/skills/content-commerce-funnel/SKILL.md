# Content Commerce Funnel

Operating pattern for brand content meant to grow revenue across **The Systems Dad (TSD)**, **MarginReset (MR)**, and **Christopher Lynn Systems (CLS)**. Adapted from Dickie Bush's four-step business system (2026-09-23). 

Always combine with **Prepare Don't Execute**: desks prepare and draft; Christopher confirms before any post, email send, or paywall publish.

---

## 1. The Chain (Do Not Skip Steps)

1. **Social Volume**
   - Capture skeletons + Systems Dad Scout draft X/LinkedIn angles; Authority long-form when the idea needs depth.
   - Cadence goal is frequent, multi-platform publishing after Christopher’s yes — not one perfect post.
   - Volume and sustained customer conversations over years beat clever one-offs.

2. **List Destination (No Orphan Posts)**
   - Every social piece and Authority package must name where traffic goes: lead magnet, newsletter signup, or a named list URL.
   - Zero orphan posts that end at “like and follow.”
   - Delivery mechanism on social: First Reply, Auto-DM trigger, or profile bio link.

3. **Nurture**
   - Outbound email (1–3×/week when active) that educates, builds trust, and proves expertise.
   - This is **not** Newsletter Desk’s keep/skip learning digest (inbound reading).
   - **Owner:** Chief of Staff routes case-by-case until Christopher names a desk — do not invent a send or assume Newsletter Desk owns outbound.

4. **Offer CTA**
   - Every nurture email and Authority package ends with one clear CTA to a paid CLS / Systems Dad / MarginReset product or next commercial step.
   - Pipeline Desk owns deal-stage follow-up after a click, reply, or inquiry.
   - **The 6-Question Offer Harness ("Write The Sales Page Anyway"):** When extracting or validating any commercial offer, answer these six questions:
     1. *Who is it for?* (Target audience, current role, specific identity)
     2. *What problem are they dealing with RIGHT NOW?* (Acute, urgent friction/bleeding neck)
     3. *What result can you help them get (benefits)?* (Concrete, observable transformation or margin dividend)
     4. *What do they get (features)?* (Deliverables, templates, call recordings, checklists, tools)
     5. *How will you help them get it (mechanism)?* (The proprietary protocol, framework, or delivery vehicle)
     6. *What's the investment?* (Price, payment terms, time commitment)
     *(Bonus proof lever: Add 2–3 specific customer testimonials or observable micro-results)*
   - **Axiom:** *"Consulting" is not the offer. A "course" is not the offer. A "coaching call" is not the offer. Those are just ways to deliver the result. The offer is how you present the problem, the result, and the reason they should choose you.*

## 2. Desk Ownership & Guardrails

| Step | Owner | Prepare-Only Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Ideas / Skeletons | Capture Desk | Never finished posts; never posts. |
| Social Drafts / Replies | Systems Dad Scout | Confirm before any post/reply. |
| Long-form + Package | Authority Desk | No silent CMS publish; package must include list destination + CTA. |
| SEO / AEO | SEO & AEO Desk | Briefs only until asked. |
| Outbound List / Nurture Sends | **Chief of Staff routes case-by-case** (no standing desk) | Never silent-send; escalate to CoS. |
| Paid Offer / Deal Stage | Pipeline Desk (+ Outbound Prospecting for first touches) | Confirm before send. |
| Brand Social Queue (Stanley) | Stanley MCP | Unapproved drafts held until Christopher confirms. |

## 4. Pre-Flight Checklist

Before marking any Authority package or Scout promo batch ready:

- [ ] **Hook / Claim is clear** (grounded in physical reality or observable friction).
- [ ] **List destination named** (lead magnet name or specific URL; no orphan posts).
- [ ] **One primary CTA** to a paid offer or next commercial step.
- [ ] **Handoff to Pipeline** if the piece is sales-adjacent.
- [ ] **Christopher’s explicit YES required** before any live post or email send.
- [ ] **Authority live-post re-check** run after live publish when Christopher confirms.

---

## 5. Anti-Patterns (Kill on Sight)

- **Social with no list path:** Publishing high-impression posts with no first reply, bio link, or lead magnet capture.
- **List growth with no nurture owner:** Collecting emails without a consistent 1–3x/week outbound cadence.
- **Nurture with no product CTA:** Sending educational newsletters without linking to a commercial next step.
- **Automating publish/send:** Breaking the "Prepare Don't Execute" boundary to "close the funnel."
- **Digest confusion:** Confusing internal reading digests with outbound customer nurture.
- **Perfection paralysis:** Skipping daily/weekly volume because the backend funnel "isn't completely finished."
