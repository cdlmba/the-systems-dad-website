# Sage Memory: Voice Examples

## The "Blink" Thread (Top Performer - April 14, 2026)
**Type**: Vulnerable Storytelling / Reality Check
**Structure**: Hook -> Personal Crisis -> Physical Signal -> Resolution/Call to Action
**Text**:
"I heard the voice through a fog.
'Are you okay?'
'Yes...'
No, I wasn't.
I remember the night clearly.
Just got done with a game night with friends.
I was there physically, but not at all mentally.
Turning over in my mind some board struggles.
And then, I just blinked.
...
Your body gives you signals when you're doing too much.
You might call it 'another season' or 'just busy!'
Your body knows.
Your body keeps the score.
You feel it in your bones.
You know you're at the red line.
I was there.
...
Today, I'm better but scarred.
I know how close to get to the line.
My wife knows when I'm silently suffering.
We talk to each other. Communication, like I tell people to do at work.
It's key. You can't let it go."
