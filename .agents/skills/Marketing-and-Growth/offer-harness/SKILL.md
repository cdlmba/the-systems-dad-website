# The 6-Question Offer Harness ("Write The Sales Page Anyway")

When stuck on *"I don't know what to sell,"* stop debating ideas, courses, or consulting packages. Open a blank document and write the sales page anyway by answering these six core questions.

---

## 🎯 The 6 Core Questions

1. **Who is it for?**
   - Specific identity, current role, and situation.
   - *Example:* "High-performing technical program managers and enterprise leaders who are working 60+ hours and missing bedtime with their kids."

2. **What problem are they dealing with RIGHT NOW?**
   - Acute, top-of-mind, bleeding-neck friction they feel on a Tuesday afternoon.
   - *Example:* "They close their laptop at 5:00 PM, but their brain is still in Slack through dinner, feeling phantom phone vibrations during family time."

3. **What result can you help them get (benefits)?**
   - Concrete, observable transformation or margin dividend.
   - *Example:* "A hard 5:15 PM mental and physical shutdown boundary, reclaiming 15+ hours a week of undistracted family presence."

4. **What do they get (features)?**
   - The concrete deliverables, tools, assets, and artifacts.
   - *Example:* "The 4:50 Firebreak protocol, 15-minute Morning Triage checklist, spouse handshake alignment script, and 90-day tracking dashboard."

5. **How will you help them get it (mechanism)?**
   - The proprietary system, framework, or delivery vehicle that explains *why* this works where generic willpower failed.
   - *Example:* "The Systems Dad Method: 5-phase capacity constraint and physical domestic environment architecture."

6. **What's the investment?**
   - Specific price, payment terms, and time commitment.
   - *Example:* "$97 one-time digital kit" or "$2,800 90-day private engagement."

*(Bonus Proof Lever: Add 2–3 specific customer testimonials, screenshots of wins, or observable micro-results).*

---

## 🏛️ The Governing Axiom of the Harness

> **"Consulting" is not the offer.**  
> **A "course" is not the offer.**  
> **A "coaching call" is not the offer.**  
>
> **Those are just ways to deliver the result.**  
> **The offer is how you present the problem, the result, and the reason they should choose you.**

---

## 🛠️ When and How to Apply

1. **Before Building Any Product:** Run this harness before writing code, creating slides, or designing landing pages. If you cannot answer all 6 questions clearly, do not build.
2. **When Drafting Sales Copy or Funnel CTAs:** Use the answers to populate the headline, subheadline, bullet points, and guarantee for the 12-Part Sales Page Framework.
3. **When Auditing Existing Funnels:** If a landing page is failing to convert, test it against the harness. Most low-converting pages sell the delivery vehicle (e.g., "12 video modules") rather than the acute problem and concrete outcome.
