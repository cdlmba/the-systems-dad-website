# Whop Spark Generator: Community Presence Architect

Use this skill to create a high-leverage engagement schedule for your **Christopher Lynn Systems** community.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operational Truth**: A high-impact leader doesn't lead through "Heroic Effort." They lead through **Systems**.

## The Weekly Spark Cycle (5-Day Template)

| Day | Focus | The "Systems Dad" Ritual |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Friday** | The Shutdown | **Protocol: The 15-Minute Friday Refresh**. Close open loops. Archive the week. Prepare to be 100% present. |
| **Sunday** | The Alignment | **Protocol: The High-Stakes Week Ahead Audit**. Identify the 3 critical meetings. Define the Wins. Sync the Family API. |
| **Mid-Week (Tue/Thu)** | Leverage Points | **Systems Move**: A prompt for a new automation, AI tool, or logic fix. |
| **Mid-Week (Mon/Wed)** | Presence Wins | **The Reflection**: Share a moment where a system bought you a presence win. |

## Documentation Standards (Whop)

1. **Length Control**: Posts must be under **1,500 characters** for Whop compatibility.
2. **The "Helpful Big Brother" Tone**: High-authority but kind. Encourage the "Manual Dad" to start small.
3. **Action-Oriented**: Every "Spark" should end with a simple community challenge (e.g., "Post your Friday Shutdown score below").

## Creative Process
1. **The Ritual Anchor**: Start with the Friday or Sunday ritual if the day is appropriate.
2. **The Systems Move**: Briefly explain the logic of the ritual (The "Why").
3. **The Simple Lever**: Give them one low-effort move they can make *today*.
4. **The Response Loop**: Ask a tactical question to spark community interaction.

## Output Requirement
1. **The 5-Day Schedule**: A list of "Sparks" by day.
2. **The High-Leverage Copy**: Ready to copy/paste into Whop.
3. **Visual Recommendation**: If an image is needed for a post (e.g., a "Shutdown" infographic).

Generate the Sparks. Multiply the Community Growth.
