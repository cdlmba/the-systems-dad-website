# Humanize (structural pass)

**SSOT:** Referenced from `.agents/AGENTS.md` → **Zero-Draft Social Copy Enforcement**. Use after drafting, before the user sees copy.

Pair with `rules/05-ai-writing-tells.md` and `.agents/skills/social-copy-filter/SKILL.md`.

**Exempt:** Tape-confirmed lines on **The Porch** (`businesses/tsd/the-porch-podcast.md`, `rules/05-ai-writing-tells.md` exceptions).

## Pangram / synthetic mirror scan

- Purge hyper-symmetrical taxonomies (identical sub-bullets, perfect 1-minute timelines)
- Purge predictable 7-beat productivity arcs
- Prefer asymmetric human weighting

## Failures to purge

- Negative parallelism stacks ("It's not X. It's Y")
- Self-clapping ("Here's the kicker", "Let that sink in")
- Artificial unbothered enthusiasm, recapping
- Signposted transitions ("Let's dive in", "Here's where it gets interesting")
- Didactic lesson labels ("Here's the lesson:", "Here's the systems lesson:")
- Writerly essay hinges ("Sitting there, I realized...", "The result was predictable:")
- AI tool hyperbole ("feels like magic", "game-changer")
- Bold-label paragraph openers ("**The problem.**")
- Invented concept labels ("The Clarity Flywheel")
- Participial tails ("..., making it a game-changer")

## Rhythm

- Vary sentence length; avoid uniform all-medium or all-staccato broetry
- Cap quotable screenshot lines at **one** per piece
- Replace em dashes with periods, colons, or parentheses

## Reader & speaking

- Write to one real colleague
- Reader leaves with a concrete step, number, or takeaway
- Specifics over adjectives
- Read aloud
