# Automation Opportunity Seeker: The "Lever" Identifying Engine

Use this skill to stop doing things manually and start building the systems that scale "Christopher Lynn Systems."

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Operational Truth**: Technology is a lever, not a savior. If the automation doesn't serve as a multiplier for you or your audience, discard it.

## The CX Leverage Audit (Priorities)

Analyze your current `WEBSITE_IMPROVEMENT_TASKS.md` and identify opportunities across these 4 CX layers.

| CX Layer | Opportunity Search | The "Systems Dad" Lever |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1 (Discovery)** | Are users manually finding your best content? | **Content Automation**: A daily "Best of Kit" bot or a LinkedIn "Hook Multiplier." |
| **Stage 2 (Conversion)** | Is the Kit -> Whop bridge a manual "Hope they click" journey? | **Funnel Automation**: A 5-Day email sequence (Kit) that "Auto-gates" the Whop purchase. |
| **Stage 3 (Delivery)** | Do new Whop members get a generic welcome? | **Membership Experience**: A personalized "System Intake" bot or an automated PDF report. |
| **Stage 4 (Retention)** | Are you manually checking for student progress? | **Community Pulse**: A bot that flags "Manual Dads" who have been inactive for >7 days. |

## Audit Methodology

### 1. The "Presence" Calculation
For every proposed automation, ask: "Exactly how many minutes of my active attention does this buy back?" 

### 2. The Logic Pass
Is the task currently done manually because the system is "Idealized" or "Reality-based"? Automate only according to reality.

### 3. The Customer Win
Does the automation save the user time? Or just you? A true **Systems Dad** move buys back presence for BOTH parties.

## Output Requirement

1. **The CX Roadmap**: A list of the Top 3 automations to install next.
2. **The "Lever" Specification**: A brief "Input/Logic/Lever/Presence" breakdown for each move.
3. **The ROI of Presence**: The total number of hours you will reclaim once the system is installed.

Identify the Levers. Reclaim the Focus. Architect the Presence.
