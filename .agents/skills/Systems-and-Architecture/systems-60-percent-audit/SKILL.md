# 60% Rule Audit: Domestic Capacity Diagnostic (Dad Edition)

Use this skill when you, your spouse, or your home feels stuck at "60% capacity." 

Look, your wife isn't "Nagging" you; she's flagging a **System Failure.** If you're walking through life like a ghost because your brain is stuck in a $100M work portfolio, it's not a failure of character—it's a failure of **Domestic Infrastructure.** Audit the system so you can reclaim the mental margin needed to stay present.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Principle 3**: Assume the system is likely capping output at 60%. Audit the system before the individual.

## The Friction Diagnostic (4-Layer Audit)

Evaluate the stuck project or role through these 4 lenses before addressing burnout or "checking out."

| Lens | The Search | The "Helpful Big Brother" Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Information Gap** | Is the "How" stuck in your head? (Wi-Fi password, mower manual, plumber's contact). | **Family OS Sync**: Move the "How" out of your brain so the home runs without you. |
| **Domestic Latency** | High-friction chores (Laundry loops, grocery supply chain). | **Process Optimization**: Remove the micro-decisions that cause "Dinner Table Decision Fatigue." |
| **Portfolio Conflict** | Is "Work Overlap" burning your margin and making you a ghost? | **Shutdown Protocol**: Build margin and set expectations before walking through the door. |
| **The "Hero" SPOF** | Are you the only one who can solve a domestic crisis? | **Architecting Redundancy**: Train the team (spouse/teens) to lead their own domains. |

## Audit Methodology

### 1. The Redundancy Scan
Is this task being done more than twice? If yes, it is a system failure, not an individual's struggle. Flag it for immediate automation.

### 2. The Truth Pass
Are the metrics for success "Operational Truth" (mapped and measured) or "Idealized Goals" (vague and stressful)? 

### 3. Presence Recall Audit (The ROI)
How much **Presence Capacity** is being burned on this domestic friction? Calculate the yield in missed bedside stories or calm dinners. Win the Home Table.

## Output Requirement

1. **The Friction Report**: A bulleted list of the top 3 systemic blockers.
2. **The 60% Diagnosis**: A clear statement on whether the issue is Systemic (Process) or Individual (Skill/Will).
3. **The Leverage Move**: A prioritized 1-2-3 punch of fixes to restore the individual to their full capacity.

Diagnose the "60% Blockage" now and restore the Leverage.
