The House Playbook

Stop the repeat questions by building one simple place for all your family's "how-to" info.

The "Look, Don't Ask" Rule

This Playbook replaces nagging with visibility. Instead of your spouse or kids asking you "where is X?", they know they can just check the board. It builds trust because everyone knows what's going on without having to pester you while you're busy.

Building the Playbook: Action Items

  • Keep it Simple: Don't try to document everything. Just pick the 3 most annoying repeat questions you get and write down the answer once.
  • Identify the "Always Questions": Think about things like the Wi-Fi password, the trash schedule, or where the spare keys are. Put them in the Playbook.
  • The New Rule: Agree with your family that the Playbook is the first place anyone looks before asking "the Dad question."
