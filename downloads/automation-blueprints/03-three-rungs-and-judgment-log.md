# 03. The Three-Rung Filter & Weekly Judgment Log

Most people quit on AI automations for one of two reasons: either they try to automate a human judgment call and clean up a mess, or they hand off so much prep work that they stop noticing when the output is slightly off.

Run any annoying weekly task through the three rungs below before you build a workflow around it.

---

## Part 1: The Three Rungs of Automation

### Rung 1: Hand Off to AI
Routine, low-risk prep where a small mistake costs nothing.
- Sorting rough notes by topic or date
- Pulling vendor phone numbers, addresses, and public hours into one sheet
- Reformatting a messy CSV or list into a clean table

### Rung 2: AI Starts, One Person Checks *(Where 80% of real savings live)*
Work where AI saves an hour of legwork, as long as one named human checks the brief before it leaves your desk.
- Turning call transcripts into written action items with owners and dates
- Comparing three insurance or contractor quotes against your hard rules
- Red-lining a draft project plan for missing dates before a review call

**The Rule for Rung 2:** Never wire Rung 2 straight to a client, boss, or family member unsupervised. AI drafts the brief; you spend five minutes checking the numbers and making the call.

### Rung 3: Never Touch with AI
Work that depends on eyes on the ground, trust, or owning a hard call in person.
- Deciding what gets cut when two deadlines collide
- Delivering hard feedback, saying no to a hallway favor, or apologizing
- Physical ground-truth checks (standing in the driveway to see if a truck actually fits)
- Conversations with your spouse, kids, or key partners

---

## Part 2: The Weekly Judgment Log

When AI starts writing your first drafts and sorting your notes, you lose the reps that used to train your gut. Keep this plain log open in a single note file. Spend two minutes on Friday jotting down one thing the AI missed in real life.

Every line you log here does two jobs: it keeps your own eye sharp, and it tells you what single rule to add to your `AGENTS.md` file next week.

| Date | Workflow Used | What AI Suggested | What Reality Showed on the Ground | Rule Added to `AGENTS.md` (if any) |
