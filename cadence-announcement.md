I've been writing to you a bit sporadically lately. 

When you are trying to build margin in your life, unpredictability is the enemy. You need a rhythm. So, we are building one here. 

Moving forward, you are going to hear from me three times a week. Each email is designed to do a very specific job for you, depending on where you are in your week. 

Here is the new cadence:

**1. Real Talk Tuesday (The Deep Dive)**
This is the flagship. You'll get one story, one breakdown of why the system broke, and three tactical moves you can make that night to fix it. This is about the friction between work and home. 

**2. Tactical Thursday (The Work-Read)**
We can't fix the tension at home if we don't fix the chaos at the office. Thursdays are purely tactical. I'll be sharing the exact program management and rules systems I use to stop acting as a "human API." If you want to stop carrying work stress into the kitchen, you need to read this one at your desk.

**3. Saturday Coffee (The 936)**
No work talk. No systems. Just a quiet moment, a photo, and a reminder of why we build the systems in the first place. 

If three emails a week is too much, feel free to just open the ones that serve you best. But if you are actively trying to take your schedule back, this is the blueprint. 

See you on Tuesday.

Let me know what you think.
