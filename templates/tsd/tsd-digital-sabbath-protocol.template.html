"Good work during the week needs real rest on the weekend. Shut down the laptop."

Your brain is still thinking about work at 7 PM on a Friday. You're physically home, but mentally you're still at your desk. This checklist helps you switch off work brain and be completely there with your family for 24 hours.

Step 1: The Friday Shutdown

FRIDAY 4:00 PM

The Goal

"Wrap up the week so you can walk through the front door with a clear head."

The Shutdown Sequence
Weekly Review Complete: Did you review what got done?
"Loose Ends" Dump: Write down unfinished ideas in your notebook or app.
Turn Off Alerts: Mute work email, Slack, and LinkedIn.
Laptop Closed: Lid shut. Out of sight. Out of mind.
CHRISTOPHER LYNN | SYSTEMS Section A: The Friday Shutdown

Step 2: Mental Reset

FRIDAY 6:00 PM
The Mental Reset

Say this out loud to yourself or your partner:

"I'm done for the week. My notes are saved. I am with my family until Monday. Everything else can wait."

The grind was never the goal.
