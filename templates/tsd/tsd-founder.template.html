A Note from
Christopher Lynn.

Most dads I talk to are winning at work. And drowning at home.

I spent 20 years helping teams inside large tech companies stay on schedule and work together. I could run a department of 50 people. But I'd walk through my front door and hit a wall of noise I had no plan for.

I was physically there for dinner, but mentally replaying Slack threads. I was the one person everyone in the house called for everything — and I had no system for it.

I didn't need another productivity hack. I needed to bring the same discipline I used at my day job into my house.

The Systems Dad is my answer to that problem. I built these things to help dads move from putting out fires every night to actually being present when it matters.

What I built for my own family:

  • ✅ The Weekly Email — One short note every Tuesday with a simple habit to try.
  • ✅ Agile at Home OS — The full set of templates and routines I use at home.
  • ✅ 1:1 Coaching — Work with me directly to fix what's not working in your week.

I don't promise perfection. I promise simple routines that help you show up — most days.

Stay Present,
Christopher Lynn
