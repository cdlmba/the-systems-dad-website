The Family Dashboard

Stop being the human search engine. Get the house logic out of your head and onto a wall or a shared screen.

If your wife or kids have to ask you "where are the band-aids?" or "what time is soccer?" you’re the bottleneck. It’s exhausting to be the only person who holds the access codes to how the house runs.

Today, we’re building your Family Dashboard. This is a single, shared place where the basic answers live—so everyone can find them without asking you. It clears out the mental drag so you can stop acting like a project manager and just be Dad.

Exercise 03: The Big Three

You don't need a complex system. You just need a single source of truth for these three areas.

01

The "Where is it?" List

Where do we keep the band-aids, batteries, sports gear, and the Wi-Fi password?

02

The Shared Calendar

Who is where, and when? Pick one place (like Google Calendar or a shared whiteboard) and stick to it.

03

The Daily Rhythms

Dinner timings, bedtime routines, and who handles what on Saturdays.

Pick Your Platform

Don't overthink this. Pick the one your family will actually use.
