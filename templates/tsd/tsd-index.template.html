“We only get 936 Saturdays before they’re grown. Make them count.”

For twenty years, my day job was fixing messy enterprise projects. At work, I helped teams talk to each other and get things done. I didn't drop balls at the office.

At home, my house was pure chaos. I walked in the front door on time, but my mind was still on conference calls and unresolved Slack threads. A few years ago, my daughter stood by my home office door holding a cheer flyer. She wanted to tell me about her practice. I didn't even look up from my laptop screen. I mumbled an answer and kept typing.

I told myself working late made me a good provider. It was just an excuse. Staring at a screen was easier than sitting down and listening.

Now my laptop shuts at 5:00. The phone stays on the charger in the laundry room. And when my daughter talks, I look right at her.

— Christopher Lynn, Senior Program Manager & Founder
