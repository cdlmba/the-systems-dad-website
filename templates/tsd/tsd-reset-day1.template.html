Finding the Leaks

Before we can build a plan to fix the house noise, we have to admit where we are dropping the ball.

I spent years walking through my front door while my head was still three miles away in a meeting room. I’d sit at the dinner table, nodding along while my kids talked, but mentally I was still drafting an email or replaying a launch schedule.

If that’s you, here’s the truth: you aren't failing as a dad. You're just operating a Work Brain that doesn't know how to shut down.

The Commitment

Tonight, when you reach the driveway—or close the laptop if you work from home—stop. Before you touch the door handle, say this out loud:

"Work is done. I am off the hook."

Take three deep breaths. Let your shoulders drop. Then go inside.
