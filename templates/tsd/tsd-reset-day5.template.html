The Weekly Reset

The 20-minute habit that keeps the win going. Systems don't fail because they're bad; they fail because life gets busy and we stop paying attention.

You’ve built four powerful shifts this week. The Morning Reset protects your start. The Dashboard reduces the noise. The Kind Ask script builds the connection. But the Weekly Reset is what keeps it all running week after week.

This is your chance to step back for a moment and see where you're heading. If you never pause to see what's working, you'll keep making the same mistakes. And if you don't plan the week, you're just waiting for the next fire to start.

Exercise 05: The Sunday 3-Step

Block 20 minutes every Sunday evening (or Monday morning) to run this checklist.

1. Look Back (What worked?)

What went well? What was a total headache? What slipped through the cracks?

2. Look Ahead (What's coming?)

Check the calendar. Any double-bookings? Any big school events? Make sure you schedule some real family time before the week fills up.

3. Make One Small Change

What's one simple adjustment that would make this week easier? Can you take a chore off your wife's plate? Change a bedtime routine?

The Finish Line

You didn't just read a bunch of self-help articles this week. You actually put practical tools in place to protect your family and your sanity. That's a huge deal. Take a second to appreciate that.

"Being present isn't something you perfect once; it's a habit you keep choosing. If the rhythm breaks next week, don't beat yourself up. Just restart at Day 1."
