Talking Better

Stop having the same argument over and over. Use a simple script that builds connection instead of walls.

At work, we have standard meetings and check-ins to keep projects on track. At home, we usually just wing it—which leads to defensive voices, quick tempers, and nothing actually getting solved.

Today, we’re learning the Kind Ask. It’s a straightforward script for bringing up stress or chores without starting a fight. It keeps you and your wife on the same side of the table.

Exercise 04: The "Kind Ask" Script

Practice using this 3-step script for one thing that's been bothering you this week.

The 3-Step Script

1. Notice (The Facts)

"I noticed I've been handling bedtime solo the last few nights..."

Avoid: "You never help..." or "You always forget..."

2. Share (The Feeling)

"And I'm starting to feel pretty burnt out."

Own your experience. Don't blame her for it.

3. Ask (The Plan)

"Can we look at the schedule and see how to split this up tonight?"

Solve it together. Stay on the same team.

The Weekly Check-In

One script won't fix everything. You need a simple rhythm. Block 20 minutes this Sunday to sit with your wife and run through these three questions:

  • 1. What went well this week? (Celebrate the wins first).
  • 2. What is stressing you out? (Just listen. Don't try to fix it yet).
  • 3. How can I support you this week? (Ask what she actually needs).
