The Morning Reset

Stop starting your day reacting to a fire drill. Give yourself 15 minutes of quiet before the world starts asking for things.

Most of us start our day by reacting. We check emails in bed, scramble for our keys, and rush out the door already behind. If you start your morning in a fire drill, you’ll stay in a fire drill until bedtime.

Today, we’re building your Morning Reset. This isn't about some complicated 5 AM "miracle" routine. It’s just about protecting a tiny block of time so you can decide what tomorrow looks like instead of reacting to it.

Exercise 02: Your 3-Block Rhythm

Pick one simple action for each column. Keep the whole thing under 15 minutes if you’re just starting.

1. Mind

What centers you?

Ex: Coffee in silence, reading 1 page, prayer.

2. Body

Get moving.

Ex: 5-min stretch, cold water, walk the dog.

3. The One Move

What wins the day?

Ex: Write down the single most important task.

The Evening Prep

The Morning Reset actually starts the night before. What’s one small drag you can clear tonight to make tomorrow morning easier?

Tonight I will: _________________________________________________

Ex: Put phone in the kitchen, prep the coffee, set out gym clothes.
