I'm Christopher Lynn.

Husband, father of two daughters, and a 15-year enterprise software delivery leader in Springfield, Missouri. I build simple systems so work stops burning out your home.

Why I build systems for home, work, and teams

For years, I ran complex corporate software programs without dropping balls at the office. At home, I was still mentally trapped in the workday—physically at the table, running through emails and next-day meetings in my head.

Willpower drains. Architecture sustains. Heroics are always a design failure.

When I hit my own wall, I stopped trying to brute-force my schedule and applied the same operating discipline I used in enterprise delivery to my own week: written intake at work, a hard 4:50 shutdown, a Sunday Kitchen Sync with my wife, and a protected weekend.

The Systems Dad started as my public notebook for testing those systems. Fatherhood is the lens I write from, not the limit of who it helps—the same principles fix a chaotic household, a packed executive calendar, or a 50-person delivery team.
