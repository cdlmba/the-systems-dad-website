The Dinner Table Script

Use these words to tell your family you are done with work for the night.

[Step 1: The Trigger]
Wait for the moment she notices you are drifting.
Give her permission to ask the question:
Are you here?

[Step 2: The Handshake]
Wait for eye contact.
Then say:
"I was answering an email, but I'm closing that loop now. I am 100% here with you. No more work tonight."

[Step 3: Phone Away]
Place your phone face down on the table or counter.
"The phone is down. I'm all yours."

Note: This isn't about perfection. I miss the trigger sometimes too. But having a simple rule makes it easy to reset when you catch yourself drifting.
