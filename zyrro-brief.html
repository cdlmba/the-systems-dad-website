Zyrro.ai: Pre-Launch Teardown Brief

Prepared for Jeff Bullas by Christopher Lynn · November 2026

What already works

Your founder note (starting online at 52, building Zyrro at 69) and the line about a container feeling a size too small both work. Anyone who has been in a long career knows that exact feeling.

1. Fix the blank social previews and the bottom form

The HTML title is still Zyrro Home with no meta description or Open Graph tags. Every time you share zyrro.ai on X or LinkedIn, the preview card renders blank.

On the Keap forms, the anti-spam honeypot and timer only attach to the top form (#signup-form). The bottom form (#signup-form-bottom) is wide open. Both forms also reuse the same field IDs (inf_field_FirstName and inf_field_Email) with no <label> tags, and there is no privacy note next to the button.

2. Clear up the AI contradiction

Right above the Anthropic AI stat on a .ai domain, the Yours card says: "No machine could have written it about you." A new visitor reads that and can't tell whether Zyrro is AI or human coaching. Just say how it works: AI pulls the patterns from your answers, and you decide what to do next.

3. Show one real example of what someone gets

The page has two images total (the logo and your headshot) and no preview of the product. The five signal pills (Visioning, Thinking, Connecting, Driving, Sensing) look clickable, yet nothing happens when you tap them and the page never explains what they mean.

Put one sample output card on the page that shows a 5-signal map and a real weekly move. People know what they are signing up for when they can see it. For the five pills, either let them open a one-line explanation or style them as plain text.

4. Tighten the mobile layout and the copy cadence

Tall hero spacing pushes the signup box below the fold on standard laptops and cuts off the orange button at the bottom of mobile (390×844). Less top padding keeps the whole form on screen. In The Arc section, five horizontal columns sit inside 676px (about 112px each), so short lines wrap six to eight rows deep. A vertical list or a two-row grid reads better.

Link the Anthropic 81,000-person stat to its source, and link your founder card to jeffbullas.com so new visitors can check your work. Cut back the "Not X. Y." phrasing across the cards too. It shows up six times, and people read that rhythm as AI.
