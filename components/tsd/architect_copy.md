### Turn Off the Work Brain

We spend all day leading teams and fixing big problems at work. 

But then we walk through our front doors and hit a wall of noise. We’re the ones everyone asks for the schedule, the chores, and the small details. 

It’s tiring. And it keeps us from being the fathers we want to be.

The Self-Serve System isn't about "working harder" at home. It's about setting up a simple plan so your house runs itself.

### The Toolkit

This isn’t another big system. It’s a simple reset that actually works:

- **The House Board** — One clear place so everyone knows what’s going on and you stop being the guy who has to fix every small thing.
- **The Sunday Sync** — Just 15 minutes to clear the house noise so you can actually rest and enjoy your family.
- **The Shutdown Ritual** — How to make sure you leave the work brain at work, so you can actually be present when you walk in the door.

Stop being the problem. It's time to be there.
