### "I don’t own this time. I’m just taking care of it."

We spend our days leading teams and fixing big problems at work.  

Then we come home and everything feels like a mess. We’re in the house, but we’re still at the office — half-listening, half-stressed, half-there.

The Self-Serve System isn’t about guilt.  
It’s about waking up before it’s too late.

Your family deserves the best version of you — the calm, organized man your team gets every day.

### The Toolkit

This isn’t another big system. It’s a simple reset that actually works:

- **The House Board** — One clear place so everyone knows what’s going on and you stop being the guy who has to fix every small thing.
- **The Sunday Sync** — Just 15 minutes to clear the house noise so you can actually rest and enjoy your family.
- **The Shutdown Ritual** — My exact steps to leave work at work so you walk through the door fully off the clock.
- **The 936 Map** — A simple way to see how much time you really have — and what matters most.

Stop walking through your own house like a guest.  
It’s time to come home — while you still can.
