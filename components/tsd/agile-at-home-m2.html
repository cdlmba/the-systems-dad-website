The Sunday Reset

Stop the 5 PM Panic by spending 15 minutes getting on the same page with your spouse.

The Secret Sauce

Don't plan for the "perfect" week. Plan for the chaos. If you know you have a late meeting on Tuesday, mark it down now so you and your spouse aren't scrambling when it happens. Planning for the friction is how you win.

Sunday Reset Action Items

  • Gather the 'Mental Clutter': Add everything from your brain to the 'Ideas' list—from that leaky faucet to the vacation you’ve been dreaming about.
  • The 5-Minute Alignment: Sit down with your spouse for 5 minutes. Pick the 3-5 things that actually *need* to happen this week.
  • Stop the Food Panic: Decide on a few meals for the week now so you aren't staring at an empty fridge when everyone is hangry.
