# I was there, but I wasn't present.

> I fixed messy projects at work for 20 years.

> But at home, I was a ghost.

I realized my family only got what was left of me after work was done. I wasn't a mean dad. I was just a busy one.

I was in the room, but my head was still running through work emails. I was physically home, mentally still in traffic. I was the guy who had to answer every question and fix every small thing while I was actually thinking about the next day's meetings.

I even found myself answering work messages during a funeral. I thought I was being responsible. I was actually just missing my life.

### The time is running out.
You only get **936 Saturdays** from the day they're born until they move out. If your child is 13, you have 260 left. If they are 15, you have 156. 

You aren't just "busy." You're spending the last few years of a season that doesn't come back.

I took how I think at work and applied it at home. Not to do more. To clear out the daily noise so I could actually sit down with my kids.

I'm still learning every day. But I'm finally coming home.
