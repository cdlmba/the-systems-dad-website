The "Systems Dad" Way: The Dashboard

Most of us feel stressed because we are the only ones who know where everything is. By setting up a simple Dashboard—a shared place for all the "house stuff"—you make sure everything runs smoothly even when you're at the office. This isn't about more work; it's about getting the work out of your head.

Your First Steps

  • Identify what matters: List the 3-5 core things that keep your house moving (e.g., Money, House Stuff, Food, Kids' Schedules).
  • Find the 'Ugh' moments: List 3 things that only you know how to do (e.g., the Wi-Fi password, the HVAC filters, the bill login) and get ready to document them.
  • Setup the Board: Create your 'Family Dashboard' using the template I've provided.
