# The AAA Tool: How to Architect a Life with Margin

### The Human Search Engine
You’ve become the on-call database for everyone around you.

At work, your team pings you every 15 minutes for a status update, a document link, or a criteria check you’ve already given twice. At home, it’s the insurance cards, the plumber’s arrival time, the guest Wi-Fi password.

You’re the answer to every question nobody bothered to write down.

When you’re administering the mundane, you can’t architect the strategic. You’re paying a Coordination Tax at work and sacrificing presence at home.

I’ve spent 15 years as a Technical Program Manager. If I hadn’t built a system to absorb those “search engine” requests, year three would’ve ended me.

Lately, I’ve been leveraging AI to help us out. I call it the **AAA Tool: Agent, Automation, Analysis.**

---

### Agent: The High-Authority Bridge
An agent is any system that acts on your behalf. Today, that’s usually AI, but it’s not about “using ChatGPT.” It’s about delegating your decision-logic.

When someone hits a friction point, they don’t just need an answer. They need a path. 

I use a prompt for messy workplace loops. Instead of spending 30 minutes drafting a careful email to a difficult stakeholder, I use an AI agent to help me differentiate the process failure from the personality conflict. Fast, clean, no collateral damage.

If you’re not using an agent to protect your composure, you’re spending your own willpower. And willpower isn’t a renewable resource.

### Automation: The Single Source of Truth
Automation isn’t code. It’s infrastructure. If you’ve answered the same question three times, you don’t have a communication problem. You have an simple gap.

I built a **Family Information Hub** to automate the domestic answers: passwords, emergency protocols, maintenance logs, key logistics. When the information lives in the hub, you buy back your presence.

At work, this looks like grounding your AI tools in your project site so the team asks the agent for a status update instead of asking you.

### Analysis: The Margin Audit
You can’t automate what you haven’t audited. Most leaders feel busy but can’t tell you where the time actually went. That’s a Capacity Leak. 

I run a **Knolling Ritual**, borrowed from artist Tom Sachs’ studio practice, to find the friction: 
*   What did I avoid? 
*   What did people ask me repeatedly? 
*   Where did the shadow meeting happen?

Analysis turns your procrastination into a diagnostic signal. If you’re avoiding a task, the system is broken. Fix the system rather than gritting your teeth and pushing harder.

---

### Why This Is a Presence Issue
The AAA Tool isn’t about doing more work. It’s about buying back margin for what matters.

When agents handle the feedback, automation handles the logistics, and analysis handles the friction, you get to actually **be** somewhere. You’re at the dinner table at 6:30. Your mind isn’t replaying the status report. Your phone isn’t buzzing with password requests.

Just you, present, at the table.

**This week:** identify one thing you’re currently acting as a search engine for. A document at work. A routine at home. Drop it into an information hub today.

**Architect the System. Win the Table.**
