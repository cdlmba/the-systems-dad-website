These first three questions are designed to remove psychological debt and initiate a massive physical and mental state change.</p><p></p><h3><strong>Question 1: Did I snooze my alarm?</strong></h3><p>Snoozing is <strong>psychological poison</strong>. If necessary, physically place your alarm across the room so you <em>must</em> stand up to turn it off.
